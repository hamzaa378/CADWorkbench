## Modeling scope

The generators use nine reusable direct-solid families: stepped shafts, hollow cylindrical bodies with flanges, bored blocks with mount holes, intersecting pipe runs, hub-and-shaft parts, radial tooth profiles, fan frames with blades, drilled plates, and finned blocks. Each named part is a dimensioned starting model. Threads, seals, certified gear profiles, internal mechanisms, sheet-metal bends, fasteners, and manufacturing details may be omitted or simplified. These solids are not editable parametric feature histories.

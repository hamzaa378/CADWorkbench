# v1.9 Production Components

v1.9 adds 100 dimensioned commands under **Create > Production Components** and a matching Create ribbon group. Each command creates one undoable direct solid from dialog dimensions. Models are illustrative starting geometries, not manufacturing-ready designs. The Wing rib creates an extruded NACA-style airfoil outline with one spar opening and two lightening holes.

## Model limits

The named parts are represented with nine reusable direct-solid geometry families. Threads, seals, certified profiles, internal mechanisms, joints, load ratings, tolerances, and manufacturing-specific details may be simplified or omitted. These parts are not certified aerospace, rail, mining, food-contact, lifting, or safety equipment.

## Geometry scope

The generators use eight reusable solid-model families: stepped shafts; annular bodies with flanges; bored blocks with mounting holes; intersecting hollow pipe runs and branches; hub-and-shaft forms; radial toothed profiles; fan frames, hubs and blades; drilled plates; and finned blocks. These are useful editable-by-dimensions starting solids, not detailed manufacturing or simulation models. Threads, seals, rolling elements, valve internals, gear tooth standards, sheet-metal folds, welds, fluid passages and material behavior are simplified or omitted. The models are direct geometry and do not create a parametric feature timeline.
